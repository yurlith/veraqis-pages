Why publish this at all

Because a verdict you cannot check is not a verdict. If a report says an entry is verified, you should be able to look up what "verified" is required to mean, which rule fired, what field it was about, and what evidence was counted — without asking us.

These definitions are also the ones our own gates enforce. false_recovered_bytes = 0 is not a slogan on a marketing page; it is a threshold that fails a build.
