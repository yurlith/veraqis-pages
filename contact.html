Contact & support

During beta, support runs by email. No account, no ticket portal, no telemetry — just a direct line.

📨 Email us

support@veraqis.tech

One address during beta, for every kind of message below. No license is required during public beta.

What to include

  • What you were doing and what happened
  • The product (Archiver / Recovery Pro) and version
  • The file format, if relevant (never send sensitive data)
  • Your OS / device

Types of enquiry

Product support

Questions about installing or using the Android Archiver, or about a Recovery capability.

Beta feedback

Recovery-result reports, bug reports and suggestions from using the beta.

Security report

Disclose security issues privately. Until a dedicated security mailbox is set up, use support@veraqis.tech.

Recovery Pro interest

Register interest in VERAQIS Recovery Pro, in development.

Please don't email us irreplaceable files. For a bug, a small non-sensitive sample or a description is enough to get started.

Recovery Pro interest

Recovery Pro is in development. No testable Android build is currently available. You may register interest in future testing and product updates — emailing us does not enrol you in a test and is not a guarantee of access, because no test programme currently exists.

Register interest

Please include:

  • Your Android version
  • Your device model
  • Your intended ZIP-recovery use case
  • Whether you'd be willing to test a future build once one exists
  • Confirmation you will not email us a sensitive or irreplaceable archive, and will not send passwords — describe the damage instead; we will not ask you to attach one
