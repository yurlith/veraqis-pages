Ledger

Experiment ledger

Nothing here is built before it is measured. Every row is an experiment with a question fixed in advance, a result, and a verdict — and the verdicts include the ones that stopped work rather than starting it. Each row names the document in the repository that holds the measurement.

How to read a verdict

GO

The pre-registered gate passed and the method was kept.

NO-GO

The measurement failed its gate, or removed the reason to build the thing at all.

LIMITED

Shipped, but only inside a scope stated on the row.

SURFACE

The addressable surface was measured. No solver was built on it.

A NO-GO is not a failure of the programme; it is the programme working. Two of the reports linked below are negative, and one of them cancelled an entire recovery engine after twelve formats were measured and the joint surface turned out to be empty.

Six fixtures built from the NIST CFTT specification's own test actions, 126 fixture-by-assertion results. 11 of 13 core assertions pass on every applicable fixture; two fail, six FAIL cells and 23 NOT_EXPOSED are published individually. Every artifact was byte-identical before and after.

On a fixture built so the key is never derivable from the payload, 100 of 100 deleted rows had their rowid reconstructed exactly from b-tree geometry alone. The same method's honest limit was measured too: inside a coalesced freeblock it resolves the identity set, not the assignment.

What holds this together

  • Measure before solver. No recovery method in the research track is built before its addressable surface is measured. No work is justified by a guessed percentage.
  • Pre-registration. The metric and its pass threshold are frozen before the run, so a disappointing result cannot be reinterpreted afterwards.
  • Zero wrong bytes is a hard gate. A method that recovers more at the cost of one wrong byte does not ship.
  • Independent verification. Evidence counts only from a verifier the solver did not target. A checksum you fitted to proves nothing — the rule.
  • Every row cites a document. The build fails if a cited source or artifact is missing from the repository, so a published claim cannot outlive its evidence.
  • Research reports — the measurements written up in full
  • The evidence model — the rules every measurement is held to
  • Test record — the release-gate checks behind the beta
