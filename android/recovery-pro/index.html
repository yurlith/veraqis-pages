Recovery on Android

This page used to describe a planned separate product called "VERAQIS Recovery Pro" for Android. That product was not built under that name, and this page now states what on-device recovery actually is instead.

Correction, 2026-08-26. There is no separate VERAQIS Recovery Pro application for Android, and none is in development. Recovery on Android is a capability inside VERAQIS Archiver Pro, and it is narrower than the desktop's. The earlier "planned Android ZIP recovery product" framing is superseded by what is described below.

What on-device recovery actually covers

Shipped in VERAQIS Archiver Pro 1.8.0, not planned.

  • SQLite databases. Rows are recovered from a damaged SQLite database entirely on the phone, in an isolated process.
  • An honest freshness verdict. The app states whether the supplied files establish the latest committed state. When it cannot prove that, it says so — and does not claim the opposite either, because "stale" would be an equally unproven claim.
  • Deleted-row identity. For a row deleted but not yet overwritten, the app can bound — and where the structure allows, prove — its original row ID, using the database's own internal b-tree structure and never its content. Where the structure cannot prove a single value, it reports a range or withholds the finding rather than guessing.
See it in the app About VERAQIS Archiver Pro

What is not on Android

  • Deep archive repair. Format-aware structural repair of damaged ZIP, 7z and RAR archives is desktop work. On Android the recovery entry point for a damaged archive reports that it is not available rather than attempting it.
  • The desktop's full evidence model. Value provenance, transaction linking, object history, journal and prior-epoch row recovery and generated explanations stay on the desktop. Only the structural row-identity layer was extended to mobile, as a specific, recorded decision.
  • Encrypted or password-protected content. Nothing here decrypts, cracks passwords or bypasses access control, on any platform.

If your problem is a damaged archive rather than a damaged database, start at verified ZIP recovery or the guide for a ZIP that will not open.
