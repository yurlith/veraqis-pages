What it does

Implemented and tested in the current build, not a roadmap.

  • Safety scan before open. A read-only scan classifies the archive — Safe, Suspicious, Blocked, Encrypted or Unsupported — before content is opened.
  • Safe listing. See what an archive contains without extracting it, with unsafe paths (traversal, absolute, reserved names) flagged rather than followed.
  • Safe extraction. Extraction is transactional and stays inside the destination you pick; entries failing a safety or integrity check are not written.
  • Verified file operations. Copy, move and extract share one destination picker. A copy is read back and its SHA-256 checked before it is committed, a move checks the source before deleting it, and name conflicts are yours to decide.
  • Reader and private files. Books and PDFs open in a built-in reader with notes and bookmarks; a private section stays behind the device PIN or biometrics.
  • SQLite row recovery, on-device. Recovers rows from a damaged SQLite database in an isolated process, states whether the current state is provably fresh, and can bound or prove a deleted row's original row ID from the database's own structure — never from its content. Details and screenshot.

"Safe" means structurally safe to extract. File contents are not inspected for malicious payloads — this is a structural safety inspector, not an antivirus.

Honest scope

  • Encrypted archives are detected and reported as encrypted. Archive passwords are not saved; VERAQIS never cracks passwords and never bypasses access control.
  • Unsupported formats are reported honestly rather than guessed at.
  • Deep archive repair is not on mobile. For a damaged ZIP the app recovers only the entries whose CRC-32 still matches, or recovers from a backup copy you select. For more, start at verified ZIP recovery.
  • Tested on emulators only. Android 8, 14 and 15 emulators; no physical phones, manufacturer skins or third-party cloud providers.
  • Passwords, when supplied, are handled as transient character data and are not written to models or logs.

Privacy and permissions

What the app can reach on your device, stated plainly.

  • No INTERNET permission is declared in the 2.10.0 manifest, so the app cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier.
  • To work as a file manager the app requests Android's all-files access. Folders on other storage providers are opened through the system picker, with the permission you grant there. It also requests biometrics for the private section and a media-playback service for read-aloud.
  • Backups are disabled (allowBackup="false") so archive contents are not swept into a cloud backup.

Distributed as a directly verifiable signed APK during beta. A Google Play listing is a separate, not-yet-started step.
