Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · applies to the VERAQIS beta

The short version: the shipped VERAQIS Archiver and the browser-based tools on this site run on your device, process your files locally, and send no telemetry. Recovery Pro has no build yet; it is being designed around the same local-first, no-telemetry model. This website is fully static — no cookies, no analytics, no trackers.

1. Your files

The shipped VERAQIS Archiver and the browser-based analysis tools on this site open the files you point them at read-only and process them locally on your device. Your file contents are not transmitted to us or to any third party, and these tools make no network calls in the course of analysis or extraction. Recovery Pro is being designed around the same local-first model, but does not exist as a testable build yet — this cannot be independently verified for Recovery Pro until one does.

VERAQIS Archiver requests the Android MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE ("All files access") permission for its built-in whole-device file manager. This is a broad permission and we do not describe it as harmless: it is requested from the storage-connection flow (not at install), and declining it does not block the app — it falls back to Android's Storage Access Framework with a per-session, single-folder picker. See the Archiver page for the full disclosure. Whichever mode is active, file contents stay on the device; granting storage access lets the app read files, it is not by itself a guarantee that nothing leaves the device — that separate claim rests on the absence of the INTERNET permission in the shipped Archiver APK, and says nothing about a future Recovery Pro APK, which does not exist yet.

2. Telemetry & analytics

There is no telemetry. VERAQIS Archiver, the only shipped application, does not collect usage statistics, crash pings, device identifiers, or behavioural data. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts. Recovery Pro is being designed to the same no-telemetry standard, once it exists as a testable build.

3. This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no third-party scripts, and includes no tracking pixels. Standard web-server access logs (if any) are kept by whoever hosts the site and are outside the scope of the applications.

4. Licensing

VERAQIS Recovery Pro is in development; no license file exists yet. Its planned license validation is designed to run offline against a signed file; it will not contact a server, create an account or transmit information about you.

5. Reports & diagnostics

Reports you generate are written to locations you choose and stay on your device unless you share them. By design, reports and benchmark artifacts record hashes, byte offsets, format metadata and quality metrics — not your raw file contents.

6. Contacting us

If you email support@veraqis.tech, we receive the information you choose to send (your address and message). We use it only to respond. Please do not send sensitive or irreplaceable files.

7. Children

VERAQIS is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

As beta software evolves, this policy may change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date. If a future feature would collect or transmit any data, it will be disclosed before it ships and will not be enabled silently.

Questions about privacy? Contact us.
