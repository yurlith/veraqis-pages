Three heroes, one team

Each hero can do something the others cannot. Most rooms need at least two of them — and the portal opens only for the whole team.

Robot · zap and glide

Vera

The team leader. Zaps drones and far-away energy nodes, and glides across gaps nobody else can cross.

Neon fox · double jump and dash

Pix

The fastest of the three. Double-jumps, dashes through the air, and slips through tunnels too low for the others.

Golem · push, pound, laser-proof

Brick

Slow and unstoppable. Pushes heavy crates, holds down heavy plates, smashes cracked floors and walks straight through lasers.

Story, bosses and missions

A story with voices

The heroes talk as they play, and a mysterious voice called Echo guides them through the dark. Who Echo really is changes everything.

Three bosses

The Warden, the Maw and the Null Queen. Each fight has three phases, and each phase needs a different hero — plates, pounds, perches and zaps.

Hard missions

Every level has one optional mission on top of its three stars: no hits, beat the clock, nobody falls, defeat every enemy or find every deep shard.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

WASD or the arrow keys walk — forward is wherever the camera looks. Space jumps; hold it for a higher jump, and as Vera keep holding to glide. E uses the hero’s ability: Vera zaps, Brick ground-pounds from the air, Pix sniffs out shards. Shift dashes as Pix. Tab or 1, 2, 3 switch heroes. Drag with the mouse or press Z and X to turn the camera; the wheel zooms. P pauses, F goes full screen, M mutes, Esc returns to the levels.

Phone and tablet

The game opens full screen. The thumb stick walks, drag anywhere else to turn the camera, pinch to zoom; the buttons on the right jump, use the ability and switch heroes.

Gamepad

Left stick walks, right stick turns the camera, the triggers zoom. A jumps, X uses the ability, B dashes, LB and RB switch heroes, Start pauses. Any standard controller connected to the browser works.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Neon Rift runs in your browser with WebGL on a computer, a phone or a tablet. There is no sign-up and nothing is uploaded; your progress stays in this browser.

How do the three heroes work together?

Switch between them at any time. A hero you leave behind stays where you left them — still standing on a pressure plate, still holding a door open. Most puzzles need two or three of them, and the exit portal opens only when the whole team stands in it.

How do I play on a phone?

Start a level and the game opens full screen. The thumb stick on the left walks, drag anywhere else to turn the camera, pinch to zoom, and the buttons on the right jump, use the hero’s ability and switch heroes.

What is the Underside?

The world below the city. In chapter 2, falling off a level does not end your run: the hero drops into the Underside, a glowing cavern with its own enemies and hidden deep shards, and rides a rift geyser back up.

What are missions?

Every level has one optional hard mission on top of the three stars — no hits, a time limit, nobody falls, every enemy defeated or every deep shard found. A completed mission adds 1,500 points and is remembered in your browser.

Will it run on my device?

It picks a graphics quality automatically and lowers it if the frame rate drops. You can also choose Auto, High, Medium or Low in the game.
