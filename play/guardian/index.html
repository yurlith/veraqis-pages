Who’s who

Vera and the creatures of the archive. Every creature stands for a real threat that VERAQIS checks for before it writes a byte.

Vera

Guardian · you

Collects only data she can prove, scans before she takes, and keeps the destination folder clean. She is the guide in every VERAQIS game.

Slipper

Zip Slip

Runs for the “../” holes to climb out of the folder you chose. At a hole it needs a moment to squeeze through — zap it first.

Rot

Bit rot

Crawls to verified data and chews it until the block turns red. Red data failed its check and is never emitted.

Bomblet

Compression bomb

Starts at ×10 and grows to ×∞. Defuse it early, or it floods the floor around it and ruins the data there.

Linker

Symlink

Opens pairs of portals that send you somewhere else — the way a symlink redirects later entries. Zap it and its portals close.

Overlap Hydra

Overlapping entries

Many heads, one set of bytes: the trick behind non-recursive zip bombs. The boss of the final level: twelve zaps, and it fires back.

Controls

Keyboard and mouse

Arrows or WASD move Vera. Space zaps — hold it to keep firing; the beam aims at the nearest creature in a straight line. E scans, P pauses, F goes full screen, M mutes, Esc returns to the levels. Click any tile and she walks there.

Phone and tablet

The game opens full screen. Drag in the stick area to move and slide your thumb to turn; hold ZAP to keep firing and tap SCAN. Tap a tile to walk there. Hold the phone either way — the map turns with it.

Gamepad

Stick or d-pad moves, A zaps (hold to keep firing), X or B scans and Start pauses. A standard controller connected to the browser just works.

What you learn here

  • Proof before recovery: grey data is only a claim until a check proves it, and red data is never emitted.
  • Zip Slip: Slippers are entries whose path climbs out of the destination through “../”.
  • Bit rot and solid archives: Rot beetles corrupt verified blocks, and in a solid chain one break loses everything after it.
  • Compression bombs and symlinks: stop a bomb while its ratio is still small; a link redirects where later entries land.
  • Overlapping entries: the Hydra is one set of bytes with many heads. The rules are on the security model.
