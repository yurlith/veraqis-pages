Questions

Is anything uploaded or tracked?

No. The games run entirely in your browser, make no network requests and have no analytics. Your progress is kept in this browser's local storage only.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. Archive Guardian has an on-screen pad and turns its map to fit a tall screen, Gatekeeper takes swipes or the two big buttons, Magic Bytes takes taps, and Bit Rot Hunt selects shards with two taps.

Who is Vera?

Vera is the guardian robot you play in Archive Guardian and the guide in every game. She stands for the VERAQIS rule: nothing is shown as recovered until it is proven.

Can I play with a gamepad?

Archive Guardian supports any standard controller the browser can see: stick or d-pad moves, A zaps, X or B scans, Start pauses.

Are the files real?

The archives, dumps and disks are generated for each round. The rules they follow — path safety, magic numbers, checksum evidence — are the real ones.
